The plan

Twelve weeks, one week at a time

Pick a week to open it. Each one has its topics, seven sessions (Day 1 to Day 7) with their own timers, and a quiz. Phases follow the three German genders: der for foundations, die for everyday situations, das for practical German.

Resources

Everything you need, nothing you don’t

Course

Netzwerk neu A1, Menschen A1, or the free Nicos Weg course from Deutsche Welle.

Vocabulary

Anki or Quizlet, 10 to 15 new words a day, always with the article.

Listening

The Slow German podcast and DW’s Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten.

Speaking

A language exchange partner on Tandem or HelloTalk, 15 minutes twice a week.

Habits that help

  • Say it out loudRead your sentences aloud every day, even when you study alone.
  • Keep a mistakes notebookWrite each error with its correction and reread it every weekend.
  • Short and dailyAn hour every day beats a five-hour session once a week.
Booked an exam or visa appointment? Count back from the date and make that week your week 12 mock exam. Shorten or stretch the earlier weeks to fit. The Goethe-Institut estimates 60 to 150 hours of instruction for A1.
