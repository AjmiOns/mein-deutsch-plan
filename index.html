Weekly rhythm

Five study days, two lighter ones

About 5 to 6 hours a week. The same pattern repeats in every week below: 60 minutes on weekdays, 45 on weekends. Write each new noun with its article: der Tisch, not just Tisch.

Resources

  • CourseNetzwerk neu A1, Menschen A1, or the free Nicos Weg course from Deutsche Welle.
  • VocabularyAnki or Quizlet, 10 to 15 new words a day.
  • ListeningSlow German podcast and DW's Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten.
  • SpeakingA language exchange partner on Tandem or HelloTalk, 15 minutes twice a week.

Habits that help

  • Say it out loudRead your sentences aloud every day, even when you study alone.
  • Keep a mistakes notebookWrite each error with its correction and reread it every weekend.
  • Short and dailyAn hour every day beats a five-hour session once a week.
Booked an exam or visa appointment? Count back from the date and make that week your week 12 mock exam. Shorten or stretch the earlier weeks to fit.
